# D2 Drafter Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Let a user bring their own AI key and have postit draft per-account post text, comment replies, and rewrites, validated against each target's limits, with no post created and nothing published.

**Architecture:**
- Tasks 1 to 3 change shared code: the data layer (provider and generation repositories, a read-only inbox lookup, the `outcome` column, audit kinds, the keyed prompt hash, config), core validation that counts the link and tags for platforms that publish one string, and a core `FrameExtractor` that `postit-media` implements.
- New `postit-drafter` (Tasks 4 to 9): the `LlmProvider` trait with two protocols, the preset catalog, the opencode-shaped config, prompt assembly, output parsing, and `DrafterService`, which owns provider management and generation. It depends on `config`, `core`, `http`, `data`, and `vault` only.
- Tasks 10 and 11 expose it: the `/ai` routes behind default-on `ai` features in `postit-api` and `postit-server`, with isolation and leak tests. Task 12 closes out.

**Tech Stack:** Rust 2024, `reqwest` through `postit-http`'s public client, `async-trait`, `serde_json`, `base64`, `hmac` + `sha2` (keyed prompt hash), `hkdf` (in `postit-vault`), `image` (in `postit-media`), wiremock, insta, sqlx tests. No new workspace dependency.

**Spec:** `docs/specs/2026-11-06-d2-drafter-design.md`. It argues from `docs/contracts/03. publishing roadmap.md` phase D2. Read it first: its tables pin the presets, the provider config shape, the routes, and the error reasons.

## Global Constraints

- Branch `d2-drafter` from `stage`. At plan time, no `*-deferred.md` or `*-verification.md` entry has a D2 trigger.
- Edition 2024, `rust-version = "1.98"`, workspace lints (`unsafe_code = "forbid"`, clippy `all` + `pedantic`; `unwrap_used`, `expect_used`, `panic` denied outside tests). American English.
- `postit-drafter` depends on `postit-config`, `postit-core`, `postit-http`, `postit-data`, `postit-vault` only, for normal and dev dependencies alike. Never on `media`, `publishing`, `oauth`, `agent-kit`, or an agent crate. Test descriptors come from the drafter's own `testkit`. Its `Cargo.toml` takes `version`, `edition`, `rust-version`, `license`, `authors`, `repository`, and `publish` from the workspace, as `contacts/Cargo.toml` does.
- Every provider request goes through `postit_http::build_public_client` with `AddressPolicy { allow_private: settings.ai.allow_private_endpoints }` and `send_with_retry` with `Idempotency::NonIdempotent`. Everything one service call sends shares one deadline: `ai.request_timeout` (default 120 s) for `generate` and `test`, `ai.request_timeout` for `models`. Task 5's `send.rs` owns how the deadline bounds attempts, sleeps, and the per-request timeout.
- Secrets (API key, header values) live in `secrecy::SecretString`, are sealed as `Sealed<serde_json::Value>` in `ai_providers.api_key` bound to `(ai_providers, api_key, id)`, and never appear in `Debug`, logs, errors, audit details, generation rows, or responses. The user's prompt and images never appear in `Debug` or logs either.
- Fixed values: prompt at most 8,000 characters (`char`s); 1 to 20 targets; at most 10 `media_ids`; at most 4 images and 4 frames per video sent; stills at most 1,568 px long side, JPEG quality 85; default `max_tokens` 8,192, lowered to the model's `limit.output` when the config gives one; no `temperature` sent unless the config sets one; test chat `Reply with OK` with `max_tokens` 5, where any successful answer counts as `ok` whatever its `finish` or text (a thinking model may spend all 5 tokens thinking); Anthropic model paging at most 10 pages; route timeout `ai.request_timeout + 5 s`; `rate_limit.ai_requests` 20 per minute, burst 5, charged once per request on `generate`, `test`, and `models`.
- `prompt_hash` is HMAC-SHA-256 keyed by `Vault::derive_key("ai_prompt_hash")`, over the owner's UUID bytes then the prompt's UTF-8 bytes. Never a bare SHA-256.
- Error codes: `ai_provider_required` (409) and `ai_provider_failed` (502, reason in `detail`: `key_rejected`, `model_not_found`, `rate_limited`, `bad_request`, `unavailable`, `timeout`, `address_refused`, `invalid_response`, `invalid_output`).
- Every human-facing string is a `postit_core::Message` keyed `ai.…` with plain English text.
- Single migration: edit `backend/crates/data/migrations/0001_init.sql` in place.
- After every task, from `backend/`: `cargo fmt --check`, `cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets --all-features -- -D warnings`, and the touched crates' tests. DB tests: `DATABASE_URL` from `!ref/vault/development/postgres.env` (quotes stripped) with host `127.0.0.1`, `-- --test-threads=3`, `CARGO_TARGET_DIR=K:/cargo-target/postit`. New `sqlx::query!` calls need `SQLX_OFFLINE=false cargo sqlx prepare -- --all-targets --all-features` from `backend/crates/data`.
- Commits: Conventional Commits, scope = crate or area, ending with `Co-Authored-By: <model that did the work> <noreply@anthropic.com>`.

## Review Focus

1. **A pasted opencode entry with `"apiKey": "{env:OPENAI_API_KEY}"`.** Refused with a violation at `options.apiKey` naming the substitution; nothing stored, nothing read from the server's environment. Test in Task 4.
2. **A base URL typed with or without the version path.** OpenAI-compatible `https://api.openai.com/v1/` and `https://api.openai.com/v1` both reach `/v1/chat/completions`, never `//chat/completions` or `/chat/completions` on the bare host. Anthropic `https://api.anthropic.com`, `https://api.anthropic.com/`, and `https://api.anthropic.com/v1` all reach `/v1/messages`, never `/v1/v1/messages`. Tests in Tasks 4, 5, and 6.
3. **The model stops at its token limit mid-JSON (`finish_reason: "length"`), or returns empty `choices`.** That is invalid output: one repair, then `ai_provider_failed` / `invalid_output`, never a 500. Tests in Tasks 5 and 9.
4. **The model answers for two of three targets, repeats one, or invents a fourth ID.** The missing target gets an `ai.target_missing` violation and triggers the repair; a repeat keeps the first; an unknown ID is dropped. Every requested target comes back. Test in Task 7.
5. **The owner deletes their default provider while a delegate has the composer open.** The delegate's next generate, with or without the stale `provider_id`, answers 409 `ai_provider_required`, not a 500 or a 404. Test in Task 8.
6. **A Telegram target whose text is 4,000 UTF-16 units, plus a link and three tags.** Core validation counts the composed string, reports `text_too_long` once, and the drafter repairs it. The publish path reports the same violation, from the engine alone. Tests in Tasks 2 and 9.

`send.rs`, which every protocol call goes through:
- The `RetryPolicy` is built per call from the time left before `deadline`: `max_attempts: 3` (at most 2 retries), `budget` and `max_retry_after` both equal to the time left, so a `Retry-After` longer than what is left returns `RateLimited` with the provider's value at once instead of sleeping past the deadline.
- Each request built by `make` gets `*request.timeout_mut() = Some(time left)`.
- The whole `send_with_retry` call, body read included, runs inside `tokio::time::timeout_at(deadline)`; an elapsed deadline is `Timeout`. A reqwest timeout comes back from `send_with_retry` as `OutcomeUnknown` (not idempotent) and is told apart by the deadline having passed: `Timeout` when it has, `Unavailable` otherwise.
- `Sensitivity::Normal` (no secret in the URL; the key travels in headers).
- Error mapping from `HttpError`: `Status` 401 and 403 `KeyRejected`, 404 `ModelNotFound`, 429 `RateLimited` (with `retry_after`), other 4xx `BadRequest`; `RateLimited` `RateLimited`; `OutcomeUnknown` (what a not-idempotent 5xx, timeout, or dropped connection becomes, status lost) `Unavailable` or `Timeout` as above; `Transient` (connect failures after the retries) `Unavailable`; `AddressRejected` `AddressRefused`; `Permanent` `InvalidResponse`. A 3xx (the public client follows no redirect) is `InvalidResponse`.
- Success bodies are read with a 4 MiB cap (`response.chunk()` loop, as `status_error` reads its excerpt); over the cap is `InvalidResponse`.
- No response body text ever goes into an `LlmError`; the reason and `retry_after` are all it carries.
